# FlowForge: Naming & Branding Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2025-01-12

---

## Brand Name: FlowForge

### Name Rationale

**Flow** + **Forge**

- **Flow**: Represents continuous, iterative development process
- **Forge**: Symbolizes crafting, building, creation through iteration

### Name Benefits

✅ **Memorable**: Two strong, simple words
✅ **Descriptive**: Suggests creating through flowing process
✅ **Domain**: flowforge.com, flowforge.io available
✅ **Unique**: No major competitors with this name
✅ **International**: Easy to pronounce globally
✅ **Verb-able**: "Let's FlowForge this feature"

---

## Visual Identity

### Logo Concept

```
╔═══╗
║ F ║ ⚡  FlowForge
╚═══╝
```

- **Symbol**: Stylized "F" in a shield/anvil shape
- **Lightning bolt**: Represents speed and AI power
- **Colors**: Primary purple gradient with teal accent

### Color Usage

**Primary Purple**: Trust, innovation, premium

- Used for CTAs, primary actions, brand elements

**Secondary Teal**: Growth, creativity, collaboration

- Used for success states, highlights, accents

**Neutral Slate**: Professional, clean, modern

- Used for UI, text, backgrounds

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Personality

- **Professional** but not stuffy
- **Friendly** but not casual
- **Confident** but not arrogant
- **Helpful** but not condescending

### Writing Guidelines

**Do:**

- Use "we" and "you" (conversational)
- Be concise and clear
- Use active voice
- Explain technical concepts simply

**Don't:**

- Use jargon unnecessarily
- Be overly formal
- Make assumptions about user knowledge
- Use negative framing

### Example Copy

❌ **Bad**: "Our revolutionary AI-powered platform leverages cutting-edge technology to optimize your development workflow"

✅ **Good**: "Build better software faster with AI agents that guide your team through proven workflows"
